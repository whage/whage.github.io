To spice up our academic publications and make them less dull I suggest replacing some of 
our "et al."-s with "atl."-s in our references. That's short for "And the lads".

Example

> The first type systems in programming language context appeared in the 1950s,
when the designers of the Fortran (Backus) and Algol-60 **(Naur atl.)** languages wanted to make
numerical computations more efficient
