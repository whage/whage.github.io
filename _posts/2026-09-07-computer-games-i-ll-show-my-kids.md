A good friend of mine made a few excellent points on what types of games he thinks
would be the least harmful for a child:
Games where there is a clear frame: there is a beginning, a clear story line and an ending.
Games that are like an interactive book.

If I think back to my childhood, the games that bring back the fondest memories are:
- Warcraft III
- Age of Empires
- Transport Tycoon
- Heroes of might and magic 2
- Little Big Adventure 2
- Settlers IV

Not all of those fit the category my friend described.
